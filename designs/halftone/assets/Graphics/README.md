Original generated graphics, all SVG; fixed inks noted per file.

- `dot-sphere` — 480×480, ~900 dots on a tilted sphere, front dots pink (#fb64fa), back dots purple-tinged (#8958da), transparent ground. Crop it by a card corner. Live, rotating version: `Halftone.sphere(canvas)`.
- `dot-glow` — 960×600, black (#0f0f0f) panel with a 16px pink dot grid swelling toward the centre and a faint pink haze. Backdrop for floating portrait tiles. Live: `Halftone.halftone(canvas)`.
- `pixel-glow` — 1200×600, a 24px mosaic of pink squares pooling from the bottom-left into black. Footer / closing CTA backdrop.
- `scanlines` — 12×12 tile of two pink dots; repeat it over photography at ~70% opacity (`ht-scanlines` does this in CSS).
- `warm-backdrop` — 1200×800 blurred terracotta/caramel studio backdrop with grain: a stand-in ground for portrait photography until real photos exist.
