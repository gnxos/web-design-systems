# GlassCards

A photo section with frosted clear-glass cards along the bottom: each with a 36px title, two lines of copy and smoked capability chips.

**Consumer provides:** a photo, 2–3 cards of {icon, title, description, 2–4 chips}.

- Markup: `section.ht-photo.ht-scanlines` > grid of `div.ht-glass` (title `ht-h2`, copy `ht-body` in `#ffffffcc`, chips `ht-tag--smoke`).
- Only over photography or the pixel glow — glass on white disappears.
