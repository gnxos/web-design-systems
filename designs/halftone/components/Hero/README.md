# Hero

A full-bleed warm photograph with a scrim, a review strip, a two-line regular-weight headline bottom-left, sub-copy, and a glass news ticker bottom-right.

**Consumer provides:** a photo (person in the right half; see Imagery), the rating line, a headline (one optional accent word in white — no pink on photos), one sentence of sub-copy, a primary + secondary button, and 1–3 ticker items.

- Markup: `section.ht-photo` (background-image = the photo) containing the text block and `.ht-ticker`.
- Text is white over the scrim; the headline uses `ht-display`.
- The nav pill floats over the top of the hero.
