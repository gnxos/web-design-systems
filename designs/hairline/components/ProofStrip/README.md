# ProofStrip

One hard number next to a quiet row of partner marks. Proof, not decoration.

**Provide:** `<div class="hl-proof">` with a `<p class="hl-proof__stat">` (the figure in `<strong>`, then one line of what it means) and a `ul.hl-proof__marks` of `hl-proof__mark` items (an SVG mark plus a name).

- The stat: `display-l` numeral, `body-l` line, in `text-strong` / `text`. Exactly one per strip; never a grid of filler stats.
- Marks sit in `text-weak` as one colour. Real partner logos keep their own colours and proportions when you have permission to show them; the preview uses invented placeholder names.

Don't: invent numbers, show more than six marks, animate the row.
