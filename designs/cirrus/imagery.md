# Imagery and illustration guide

Cirrus pictures are airy and luminous: painterly skies, soft clouds, deep night with light streaks, pale blue-lilac mesh grounds, frosted glass over all of them, and clean white product mock-ups. All images in `assets/` are original and regenerated by the recipes below. No photographs of real people, no stock office scenes.

## The kinds of picture

| Kind | What it is | Where it goes | Asset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Day sky** | A deep-to-pale blue vertical gradient with soft white cumulus low in the frame and a pale haze at the bottom. | Hero field, feature cards, CTA | `Skies/sky-day.svg`, `sky-card.svg` |
| **Dusk sky** | Navy to violet to a peach horizon, with peach and lilac clouds. | Feature cards, testimonial grids | `Skies/sky-dusk.svg` |
| **Night streaks** | Near-black navy with 20–30 thin diagonal light streaks (blue, violet, pink, amber, mint) and fine star dust. | Announcement banner, dark fields | `Skies/night-streaks.svg` |
| **Mist mesh** | Pale blue ground with five large blurred blobs (lilac, sky, periwinkle, azure). No texture. | Behind product mock-up cards | `Skies/mist-mesh.svg` |
| **UI mock-up** | A white window with abstract rows, a flow of pill nodes joined by a line, or a table with role chips. No real text. | Inside mist fields and feature cards | `Panels/ui-flow.svg`, `ui-table.svg` |
| **Avatar** | A flat head-and-shoulders silhouette on a pastel gradient, 220×220. | Testimonials, table rows | `Panels/avatar-a/b/c.svg` |
| **Pattern** | A fading grid or dot field, ink at 12–40% opacity. | Quiet section grounds | `Patterns/grid-fade.svg`, `dots-fade.svg` |

## Rules

1. **Sky first.** Most large surfaces carry a sky: clean horizon, soft clouds, no hard edges, no buildings, no hills, no figures.
2. **Frosted glass over everything.** Text over art sits on a frosted panel (50px blur) or a glass card (20px blur, 50% white edge). Art alone never carries text.
3. **Keep a scrim.** White text on a bright sky needs the `photo-scrim` (a bottom-weighted 40–65% black gradient). Check contrast on the real crop.
4. **Mock-ups are clean white.** Product cards are white with 16px corners, a 4px/9px soft shadow, and invented content.
5. **One focal point.** One cloud bank, one streak field, one halo per frame.
6. **Never** use real customers' faces, logos or company names; never draw a logo; avoid planets and robots.

## How to make each one

- **Day sky (SVG).** 1600×900 `linearGradient` `#0a4fc7 → #4f9df0 → #cfe6ff`. Clouds: 8–10 groups of four white ellipses (rx 120–300, ry 30–80, opacity 0.55–0.9) in the bottom half, passed through a filter: `feTurbulence fractalNoise 0.011 / 4 octaves` → `feDisplacementMap scale 90` → `feGaussianBlur σ 9`. Add a pale `#e8f3ff` blurred ellipse (σ 90) at the horizon. Use `filterUnits="userSpaceOnUse"` with a very large region so no square edge appears.
- **Dusk sky.** Gradient `#101e55 → #4e4fa0 → #e58f78 → #ffd3a2`; peach clouds low, lilac clouds high at 25–50% opacity; a warm glow at the horizon.
- **Night streaks.** Gradient `#050a1f → #0a1a4a → #14122e`. 26 lines, 260–780px long, angle about −18° ±4°, stroke 1–5px round caps, opacity 0.25–0.8, blurred σ 3. Add 160 dust dots (0.5–1.4px, white, 20–70%).
- **Mist mesh.** Base `#cfe3ff`; five circles r 260–320 in `#b9b2ff #7cc7f0 #a6b8ff #69b6f4 #d4c8ff`, blurred σ 90.
- **Mock-ups.** White 24px-radius canvas; pills 36px tall with a dot and a 10px bar; rows of grey bars at 40–60% ink; one blue 32px square for a "document" node.
- **Avatar.** 220×220 pastel gradient; shoulders path in one flat colour; a circle head in a skin tone. No facial features.

## AI image prompt (copy and paste)

```
A wide cinematic sky, soft and luminous. Deep cobalt blue at the top fading to pale sky blue near the horizon. A few large, fluffy white cumulus clouds in the lower half with gentle shading, softly blurred edges, atmospheric haze at the bottom. Painterly digital illustration, calm and optimistic, clean negative space in the upper middle for a headline. No horizon objects, no buildings, no people, no text.
```

Variants: replace the palette with "navy, violet and a peach horizon glow, peach and lilac clouds" (dusk), or "near-black navy with thin diagonal light streaks in blue, violet, pink and amber and tiny stars" (night).

## Negative prompt

```
people, faces, hands, buildings, planets, robots, logos, text, letters, watermark, hard edges, outlines, harsh shadows, 3D render, glossy plastic, neon grid, heavy grain, vignette, lens flare, stock photo, clutter, busy foreground, anime characters
```

## File rules

SVG with a single `viewBox`, no external references, no fonts and no `<style>` blocks. Filters declare `filterUnits="userSpaceOnUse"` with a large region. Single-ink patterns use `#1d1d1f` (an `<img>` cannot inherit `currentColor`); in Dark apply `filter: invert(1)`. Name files `kind-variant.svg`.
