# Imagery

Pictures are technical drawings: thin, flat, isometric and honest. No photographs, glossy 3D or logos.

| Kind | How to make it | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isometric parts | 30 degree projection, 1px grey strokes, white faces, teal on one part only | `assets/Drawings/` |
| Exploded stack | Three to five thin slabs with small modules, evenly spaced, labels to the right | `layer-stack` |
| Trace field | Right-angle and 45 degree traces from both edges into two small modules; a few square teal nodes | `trace-field` |
| Character fields | Small mono digits as a skyline or a disc, fading with height | `assets/Fields/` |
| Tick dials | Two rings of short ticks over 140 degrees, teal ticks up to the value | `assets/Gauges/` |
| Textures | 5px dot grid or 6px hatch in 25 to 35% grey | `assets/Patterns/` |

## Prompt for new images
> Isometric technical line drawing of [subject] on pure white, uniform 1px light grey strokes, flat white faces, a single part filled in mint teal, generous empty space, precise geometry, no shading, no text.

## Negative prompt
> photographs, 3D render, gradients, drop shadows, logos, brand marks, readable text, people, neon, dark background, hand-drawn wobble, clutter
