# AskBar

The AI prompt input: a white field with a powder border and soft powder glow, a round ink send button, and a powder sparkle hovering above-left.

**Consumer provides:** placeholder question, the submit handler, an accessible label.

- The `glow-ai` shadow is reserved for this component — nothing else in the system glows.
- Place it on paper or inside a white product card, centred in its pane, max 460px wide.
- The sparkle twinkles (`.ik-twinkle`) — it is the system's sign for AI.
