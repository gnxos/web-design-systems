# Imagery

Pictures are dim, precise and made of light, lines and paper. Nothing glossy, no stock handshakes, no logos.

| Kind | How to make it | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light beam | Black ground, one radial falloff from just above the top-left, 30 to 35% white at the source, 40 to 100 dust dots of 1 to 3px | `assets/Light/` |
| Constellation | 60 to 90 nodes in five or six loose clusters, 0.6px links to the two nearest, a quarter of nodes drawn as small flat squares, circles or triangles in series colours | `assets/Networks/` |
| Report cover | Off-white sheet with a 10px grid, five punched holes on the left, a second sheet offset 6px, two or three rectangles filled with a diagonal gradient and vertical grain, serif title near the foot | `assets/Reports/` |
| Charts | 1.6px lines or narrow 18px rounded bars on faint rules, no fills, no legends inside the art | `assets/Charts/` |
| Story media | Warm or cool defocused room, one subject, dark foot for text | `assets/Media/` (abstract stand-ins) |
| Avatars | Flat head and shoulders, two tones, no face | `assets/Avatars/` |

## Prompt for new images
> Dark editorial technology image. Pure black background, a single soft grey light entering from the top-left, fine dust, thin 0.6px connecting lines between small flat nodes, restrained accents of cobalt, mint, peach and violet, large areas of empty black, crisp and quiet, no text.

For report covers:
> Flat scan of off-white gridded paper with punched holes down the left edge, two or three rectangles of grainy vertical-striped gradient (deep navy to amber, or teal to moss), generous blank space, no text.

## Negative prompt
> logos, brand marks, readable text, real people's faces, neon cyberpunk, purple-to-blue gradients, glossy 3D, lens flare, busy backgrounds, stock photography smiles, watermarks
